## Actual counters, not occupancy

[Top wiring](/home/coyang/zynq-parrot-linux-borrow256-profile/cosim/black-parrot-example/v/top_zynq.sv:470) appends three read-only GP0 words without moving old offsets or adding a BP/kernel CSR. Each packs I in bits15:0 and D in31:16. Observer9c's compiled GP0 base is0x40000000, so the physical ARM addresses are:

| Offset / address | I/D event |
|---|---|
|0x3c /0x4000003c|Accepted retained response: valid AND ready AND hit, with enable, no retained clear or demand mutation|
|0x40 /0x40000040|Successful insertion publication AND insertion done; not merely an attempted capture|
|0x44 /0x40000044|Accepted normal L1 SRAM write packet while UCE is in retained-fill state22; actual slow-write enable required; I abort-inflight excluded|

The [counter module](/home/coyang/zynq-parrot-linux-borrow256-profile/cosim/v/bp_retained_use_counters.sv:1) samples I on positive ACLK and D on negative ACLK. All six fields saturate at65535. They reset only with external AXI reset, are gated while BP reset is asserted, and survive CPU stop, tag reset, retained clear and mode0 control. There is no software counter-clear, hardware snapshot latch, per-address/context identity, occupancy/eviction count, lookup-miss denominator, or separation of demand-copy versus prefetch publication. A configured256/1792 slots is not measured occupancy or unique useful retained bytes.

A hit is accepted mechanism use, not proof of user-visible benefit or final instruction retirement. I may abort later, so one hit need not produce eight writes; writes should not automatically be divided by eight into useful lines. Publications can repeat addresses and include kernel, allocator and child activity. The [UCE insertion mux](/home/coyang/zynq-parrot-linux-borrow256-profile/import/black-parrot/bp_me/src/v/cce/bp_uce.sv:870) admits ordinary demand-copy and eligible full-prefetch insertions. Source-none PREFIX/REPLAY evidence is therefore needed to attribute a diagnostic to ordinary copy. These counters do not measure avoided DRAM bytes or service latency.

Once any field is saturated, per-cell before/after reads CANNOT recover that cell's exact count; the field is a lower bound. Equal saturated readings do not establish inactivity. Never wrap/subtract65535 as though the hardware wrapped, discard saturated/null outcomes, or select shorter runs after seeing effects.

## Sampling options without reinterpreting the primary estimator

1. **Separate one-case post-stop diagnostic:** existing9c can observe an unchanged COST ELF after one workload/size/mode per fresh AXI-reset boot. A NEW diagnostic collector must bind9c, require exact source/artifact/46MiB/return/recovery/stability proof, run only that case, and avoid a contaminating pre-case probe in the counted boot (capability can be read nonmutating; mandatory physical qualification remains separate). This preserves the main COST guest/estimator and does not create a performance comparison. Current strict collector cannot express this role.
2. **Command-level snapshots:** a new host read-only observer service and collector could sample while Linux is back at the existing shell barrier, before each command and after its verified return. No guest change is needed. Counts aggregate that command's off/on cells; with proven mode0 off cells and source-none, they can witness enabled mechanism use collectively, not assign a count to each WHOLE pair. A declared stable nonsaturated difference requires both endpoints and no AXI reset.9c as implemented lacks this service.
3. **Per-cell snapshots:** a separate guest must explicitly wait at keyed before/after barriers outside the478–494 WHOLE interval, with mode0/UCE-backend idle and source-none at both. The collector samples/acknowledges before allowing the next cell. Merely reacting to a printed WHOLE line is racy: target UART buffering can let the next cell begin before the ARM read. Control0 does not erase GP0 history, so ending reads may follow reset/poll. New guest code changes ELF/layout and cache history even if the WHOLE definition stays fixed; use a distinct protocol/pilot and never relabel its timing as the frozen primary COST result. Samples or host waits INSIDE WHOLE change the estimator and must not be “corrected” by subtracting guessed observer cost.

## Requested short natural diagnostic: feasibility and bound gap

A fixed diagnostic design would cover all five existing workloads (scan/lookup/parse/chase/real regex),32/128KiB corpora and ordinary I/D/joint, separately for both capacities: one workload/size/mode per fresh AXI-reset boot, no chosen addresses, no effect-dependent stopping or extra samples. Exact guest checks, real returns and all six raw fields/stability/saturation flags would be mandatory. The short run supplies no speedup percentage; the complete primary application study remains efficacy evidence.

The current guest CLI accepts minimum loops1/repeats1/warmup1, which yields one off and one on cell. The on cell executes2 actors ×(1warm+1timed)=4 workload passes. These source-derived bounds cover ONLY main target-data work, not I execution, allocation/regex compilation/checks/free, kernel/futex/fork/polling or interrupts:

| Workload | Minimum-on-cell structure / conservative target bound |
|---|---|
|Scan|4×B/64 byte loads:2048 at32KiB,8192 at128KiB. Loose worst case of one retained restore/load gives16384 or65536 word writes. The128KiB bound already fails to certify strictly below65535; this is NOT a prediction all loads hit or saturation occurs.|
|Lookup|4×64 queries; each source loop permits up toB/16 slot probes plus a value read. That loose bound is524544 /2097408 target loads. Half occupancy and deterministic probe analysis could tighten it, but no accepted complete event/retirement bound is currently supplied.|
|Parse|4 complete variable-record scans of up toB bytes, with repeated volatile byte tests. Spatial locality helps normal L1, but a byte-count or configured capacity is not an all-traffic retained-event bound under interference.|
|Chase|4×64 nodes ×two target loads=512 target loads (both sizes), giving a loose4096 target write-beat bound before all other work.|
|Regex|4×64=256 regexec calls, plus two actors' six regcomp calls, descriptors, opaque/lazy libc allocation, full input/metadata checks and regfree. Call counts are not retired-instruction or retained-event bounds.|

These follow [resume_workload.h](/home/coyang/zynq-parrot-linux-capacity/linux-tests/resume_workload.h:236) and [regex workload](/home/coyang/zynq-parrot-linux-capacity/linux-tests/retained29_regex_workload.h:139). Since COST enables before allocation and disables only after checks/free, bounding only the timed user loop is insufficient. No supplied bound covers all I/D events from Linux/interrupt activity or libc regex internals. Consequently **a fixed below65535 guarantee for all six fields, all five workloads and both capacities is NOT currently established**. A failed conservative bound is not evidence of actual saturation; do not invent a tighter number or infer it from slots. Both capacities share the six16b counter limits.

A minimal separate **diagnostic guest/protocol version** could keep allocation/fork/pattern compilation/check/free in mode0, support one fixed enabled work interval with zero active warmup (current CLI forbids zero), use the same existing generators/query counts and verify all results afterward. One full scan pass per actor would reduce the target-only128KiB scan bound to4096 restores/32768 writes; lookup and regex still need actual instruction/library/kernel bounds. This narrows attribution but does not by itself prove global nonsaturation. No such version is implemented or admitted here. An existing-CLI short candidate may instead be preregistered only as qualitative, with mandatory actual nonsaturation qualification/failure reporting and NO adaptive shrinking/retry; it must not be presented as the requested proven bound. No RTL/counter-width change is required or authorized during the frozen bisection.

## Interpreting a neutral application result

More publications do not mean more occupancy or successful reuse. Zero scoped hits can mean normal L1 served the work, no reuse after capture, invalidation/collision loss or other interference; the counters do not distinguish these causes. Positive hits with real writes establish mechanism use but not benefit; neutral WHOLE can still include control/lookup/copy costs and OS/setup cost that outweigh useful retention. Existing resource rows help describe faults/system time without attributing a causal saving. Report zero, negative, saturated and unstable diagnostics without filtering, and keep them separate from primary efficacy outcomes. Same-hardware mode0/I/D/joint plus source-none distinguishes ordinary retained-capacity use from active prefetch; counters alone cannot make that distinction.
