This pretarget addendum narrows the interpretation of the fixed natural-retention experiment. It changes **no acquisition slot, flag, endpoint, analysis rule, preregistration, ELF or runner**, and uses source only, with no primary-data read or target execution. The frozen admission SHA-256 remains `2b516262d5e36e81b93ad375910da89a452a43f46d5a2d156064f3fd2bf76f02`.

The disabled arm is an **instrumented, matched mode0 control**, not unmodified Linux deployment. `retention_start_cell()` runs unconditionally in each compiled cell, selecting mode0 when disabled and mode1/2 when enabled. Both arms call the same policy control/status sequence, and both end with status, global control0 and reset readback before scoped prefix cleanup, ending resource observations and the WHOLE timestamp. Actual kernel27 control performs `csrw 0x5c1` and `fence.i` for every accepted mode, including zero. Therefore both arms pay the per-cell control/fence/status and observer costs; the disabled arm also incurs operations that ordinary unmodified execution would not require. These are **per-cell lifecycle operations, not per-wake calls**. Startup and reporting remain outside the frozen per-cell WHOLE boundary.

A positive paired WHOLE benefit prices the enabled path inside that boundary and can support **retention-versus-mode0 under the matched lifecycle**. It does not by itself establish a net gain over unmodified Linux deployment. Shared fences and observation also alter cache/lifecycle state, so the missing deployment baseline is not recoverable by subtracting a scalar measured syscall cost. This is the same distinction between matched mechanism controls and unmatched deployment cost highlighted by the earlier RL122 caveat, applied here at cell rather than wake granularity.

The mode0 contrast can remain a valid comparison of changed use of retained capacity under that stated boundary; it does not establish guaranteed unique extra capacity or prefetch effectiveness. All negative and null outcomes remain mandatory, and no runtime-plan expansion follows from this clarification.

If a later positive result motivates a deployment claim, that claim requires a **separately authorized, preregistered and actually executed unmatched lifecycle baseline** before acceptance. No subtraction, extrapolation or projection of separately measured policy/syscall costs may manufacture that net-gain result. No such baseline, new flag or additional execution is added now.

This does not invalidate a separately controlled mode14 kernel A/B policy comparison: when both variants use the same ABI, enabled-control preparation and measured boundary, their difference can identify the changed kernel policy under those controls. Absolute enabled-arm WHOLE is a required cross-check against a paired-benefit change caused by movement of the off arm.
