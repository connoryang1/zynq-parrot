This isolates the retained-maintenance combinational feedback reported by actual Vivado synthesis of the immutable1280 r2 candidate. The fix preserves the existing request acceptance and same-cycle clear semantics while removing maintenance's dependence on the mixed prefetch/ordinary `yumi` output. It does not change capacity, ordinary L1 geometry, policy or memory timing.

In e109, UCE clear depends on its maintenance output, directly and through the top-level lane clear. Prefetch acceptance depends on clear, while maintenance is computed from the final mixed request acceptance. Legal prefetch and maintenance opcodes are exclusive, so the feedback is not sensitizable for a stable legal opcode, but the synthesized structural timing loop is real and must be removed rather than waived. The new maintenance pulse uses the ordinary ready-state/FIFO acceptance and the final installer suppression: maintenance-valid, ready state, FIFO ready, no previously accepted request, and no installer ownership unless demand priority admits the current request. The common final-yumi combinational block remains byte-identical. A runtime assertion checks exact equality to final-yumi qualified maintenance after reset.
