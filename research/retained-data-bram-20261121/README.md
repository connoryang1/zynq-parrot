Full replay/preview mode requires actual64B transport, `prefetch_full_line_p=1`, critical-sector forwarding0 and old cache-local side buffering0. It never treats a16B reply as a full line. A completed packet uses the existing pipeline/write-buffer drain and all-way normal-tag scan; **any normal-resident match is dropped**, covering dirty L1 data that may be newer than L2. The short validation interval excludes cache traffic, including otherwise hot hits, and insertion/lookup arbitration adds real cost. This is not free capacity. A store cancels matching pending captures and owned packets. A dirty victim may leave L1 before an old L2 reply arrives, so actual ordinary writeback handshakes also cancel matching owned preview packets; a later resident scan alone would be insufficient. Sent cancelled packets retain their transport ownership until every remaining reply beat drains. An ordinary nonblocking write has explicit priority over a queued preview and cannot be falsely retired while its forward packet is replaced.
